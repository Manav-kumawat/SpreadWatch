July 19, 2024 | Mumbai
SYN Issuer 07 Ltd

Rating downgraded to 'SYN A/Negative' from 'SYN AA-/Stable' on the Non-Convertible Debentures of SYN Issuer 07 Ltd.

Rationale

The rating action follows a significant increase in gross non-performing assets in the wholesale lending book and a slowdown in collections over the last three quarters. SYN-AGENCY-A will continue to monitor the refinancing plan and the liquidity position closely.

Key rating drivers

Liquidity: the company had cash and equivalents that cover only part of its scheduled repayments over the next twelve months.
