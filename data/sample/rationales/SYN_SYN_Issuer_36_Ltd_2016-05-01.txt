SYN Issuer 36 Ltd

Rating downgraded to 'SYN A-/Negative' from 'SYN A/Stable' on the Non-Convertible Debentures of SYN Issuer 36 Ltd.

Rationale

Delays in receiving payments from state distribution utilities have stretched the working capital cycle and weakened debt service coverage. SYN-AGENCY-A will continue to monitor the refinancing plan and the liquidity position closely.

Key rating drivers

Liquidity: the company had cash and equivalents that cover only part of its scheduled repayments over the next twelve months.
