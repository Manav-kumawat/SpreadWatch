Rating Rationale
April 10, 2016 | Mumbai
SYN Issuer 35 Ltd

Rating downgraded to 'SYN BB/Negative' from 'SYN BBB/Stable' on the Non-Convertible Debentures of SYN Issuer 35 Ltd.

Rationale

Refinancing risk has risen materially as access to commercial paper and bank lines has tightened, and the company has relied on asset sales to meet maturities. SYN-AGENCY-A will continue to monitor the refinancing plan and the liquidity position closely.

Key rating drivers

Liquidity: the company had cash and equivalents that cover only part of its scheduled repayments over the next twelve months.
