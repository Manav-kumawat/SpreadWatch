Rating Rationale
January 02, 2017 | Mumbai
SYN Issuer 18 Ltd

Rating downgraded to 'SYN AA-/Negative' from 'SYN AA/Stable' on the Non-Convertible Debentures of SYN Issuer 18 Ltd.

Rationale

Operating margins have contracted because of higher input costs, pushing net leverage above the level expected for the earlier rating. SYN-AGENCY-A will continue to monitor the refinancing plan and the liquidity position closely.

Key rating drivers

Liquidity: the company had cash and equivalents that cover only part of its scheduled repayments over the next twelve months.
