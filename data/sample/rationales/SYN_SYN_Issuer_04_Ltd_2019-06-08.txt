Rating Rationale
June 08, 2019 | Mumbai
SYN Issuer 04 Ltd

Rating downgraded to 'SYN A+/Negative' from 'SYN AA/Stable' on the Non-Convertible Debentures of SYN Issuer 04 Ltd.

Rationale

The downgrade reflects a sharp weakening in the liquidity profile of the company, with large debt repayments falling due over the next two quarters against limited unencumbered cash. SYN-AGENCY-A will continue to monitor the refinancing plan and the liquidity position closely.

Key rating drivers

Liquidity: the company had cash and equivalents that cover only part of its scheduled repayments over the next twelve months.
