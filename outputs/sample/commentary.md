On the research question - whether credit spreads on corporate bonds move before the rating agencies act - the engine's answer on this dataset is YES [E:es.verdict]. Across the sample, 37 of 60 downgrade episodes had enough trades to measure [E:es.events], and the mean abnormal widening in the pre-event window was 121.5 bps [E:es.mean_pre]. The widening is tested against zero with a one-sided t-test t = 3.08, p = 0.0020 [E:es.ttest]. As a signal, the early-warning rule fired before 54.1% of downgrades, while the same rule fired on 4.2% of 48 random placebo dates [E:es.hit_rate][E:es.false_alarm]. In those episodes the median lead time when it fired was 28 days [E:es.lead]. Measured against the full move, the median share of total widening that happened before the action was 82% [E:es.pre_share].
